DMCA Takedown Notice

Effective immediately — LA BOVEDA by Natanael Cano has been removed from N3XT.

What happened?

We received an official DMCA (Digital Millennium Copyright Act) takedown request regarding the album LA BOVEDA by Natanael Cano. The request was filed by LOS CT, the rights holders and management behind the artist.

Who filed the request?

LOS CT — the official label and management team representing Natanael Cano — submitted a formal copyright infringement notice claiming that the distribution of LA BOVEDA through N3XT violated their exclusive rights under 17 U.S.C. § 512(c) of the Digital Millennium Copyright Act.

What content was affected?

The following content has been fully removed from N3XT as a result of this notice:

· Album: LA BOVEDA — Natanael Cano (2026)
· All 18 tracks including audio previews and metadata
· Cover art and any associated promotional material
· Download access previously granted to authorized users

Why did this happen?

LA BOVEDA was an unreleased album that had been circulating through unofficial Telegram channels before its official release. N3XT sourced this content from those community channels in good faith for informational purposes. However, upon receiving the DMCA notice from LOS CT, we are legally obligated to remove the content immediately and comply fully with the request.

Our response

N3XT takes copyright law seriously. We have fully complied with the takedown request and removed all associated content within the legally required timeframe. We do not contest this notice.

A message from N3XT

We sincerely apologize to our users who were accessing LA BOVEDA through our platform. We understand this is disappointing, especially for fans of Natanael Cano. We also apologize to LOS CT and Natanael Cano for any harm caused by the unauthorized distribution of this content. This was not our intention, and we are committed to respecting the rights of all artists and their teams.

If you believe this removal was made in error or wish to submit a counter-notice, please contact us directly.
